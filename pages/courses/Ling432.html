About This Course

This upper-division course introduces students to the core methodological approaches used in [Your Field]. Students will learn to design research projects, collect and analyze data using both qualitative and quantitative methods, and present findings in scholarly formats appropriate to the discipline.

By the end of the semester, students will have completed a small-scale original research project and a methods paper ready for further development. The course emphasizes research ethics, reflexivity, and the relationship between theory and method.
